Spain Student Visa for Canadians: Complete 2026 Guide

Canada is one of Spain's largest sources of language school and university students. This guide covers everything Canadian citizens need to know — which Spanish consulate to use, how to get your RCMP check apostilled, financial proof requirements, and a step-by-step application process.

Can Canadian Citizens Apply for a Spain Student Visa?

Yes — and the process is well-established. Spain actively welcomes Canadian students at language schools and universities.

Canadian citizens are non-EU nationals, which means a Type D student visa is required for any course or programme lasting more than 90 days in Spain. This is a national long-stay visa that permits you to reside in Spain for the duration of your studies.

For shorter stays — a summer intensive course, a holiday language class, or any programme under 90 days — Canadian passport holders can enter Spain visa-free under the EU/Spain–Canada bilateral agreement. No visa is needed for those visits.

ℹ

Key rule: If your programme, course, or total intended stay in Spain exceeds 90 days within any 180-day period, you need a student visa before you depart Canada. You cannot switch from tourist status to student visa once inside Spain.

Canada has strong cultural and educational ties with Spain. Barcelona, Seville, and Granada are the most popular cities with Canadian students, particularly those enrolled in Spanish language schools and university exchange programmes.

What Can Canadian Students Study in Spain?

The student visa covers a wide range of academic and vocational programmes.

Spanish Language Schools

The most common route for Canadians. Long-term language courses (DELE preparation, general Spanish, business Spanish) at accredited schools in Barcelona, Seville, Granada, Valencia, or Madrid. Courses must be at least 90 days to require a visa.

University Degree Programmes

Bachelor's, Master's and PhD programmes at Spanish universities. Popular for Canadians pursuing programmes in arts, architecture, design, business, and medicine. You'll need an official acceptance letter from the institution.

Vocational & Professional Courses

Culinary arts, flamenco, architecture, film, music conservatory — Spain has a strong vocational sector. If the programme is officially recognised and lasts 90+ days, it qualifies for a student visa.

Exchange Programmes

Many Canadian universities have exchange agreements with Spanish institutions. If your Canadian university arranges the exchange, they can often help with documentation.

Financial Requirements for Canadian Applicants

Spain requires evidence that you can support yourself throughout your studies.

The standard requirement is approximately €600 per month for the duration of your stay (roughly €7,200 for a full academic year). This does not have to be held in euros — Canadian bank statements in CAD are fully accepted. The consulate will assess the equivalent value.

How Canadian Bank Statements Are Assessed

  • Provide 3–6 months of recent bank statements showing consistent balances
  • Statements should show your account number, name, and the issuing bank's letterhead
  • Online banking PDFs are generally accepted; certified/stamped statements from the bank are stronger
  • The consulate looks for a stable balance well above the minimum — aim for at least CAD equivalent of €10,000+ for a year-long stay
  • Superannuation or RRSP accounts: these are not typically counted as they represent locked-in or retirement funds, not accessible liquid assets
  • Parents sponsoring the student should provide their own statements plus a signed financial support declaration
⚠

Important: Tuition fees are separate from living expenses. The €600/month figure covers accommodation and living costs only. If you have already paid tuition, include proof of payment — this strengthens your application considerably.

Documents Checklist for Canadian Applicants

You will need the standard student visa documents plus Canada-specific items.

Canada-Specific Documents

  • RCMP Criminal Record Certificate — obtained through the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, apostilled by Global Affairs Canada
  • Canadian passport — valid for at least 1 year beyond your course end date, minimum 2 blank pages
  • Canadian bank statements — 3–6 months, in CAD, showing sufficient accessible funds
  • Provincial medical certificate — from a registered Canadian physician confirming no communicable diseases

RCMP Criminal Record Check — Step by Step

  • Apply at rcmp-grc.gc.ca or through an accredited RCMP fingerprinting service
  • Choose a Certified Criminal Record Check (not a vulnerable sector check)
  • Processing time: typically 2–4 weeks (expedited available)
  • Once received, it must be apostilled by Global Affairs Canada
  • The apostille confirms the document is genuine for use in Spain

Global Affairs Canada Apostille

Canada joined the Hague Apostille Convention in January 2024. Documents can now be apostilled through Global Affairs Canada (Authentication Services) in Ottawa. This replaced the previous authentication + consular legalisation process and is significantly simpler and faster.

  • Submit the original RCMP certificate to Global Affairs Canada Authentication Services
  • Standard processing: approximately 10–20 business days
  • Expedited service may be available

Standard Student Visa Documents (All Nationalities)

  • Completed national visa application form (EX-01)
  • Recent passport-size photos (2, white background, EU specifications)
  • Acceptance/enrolment letter from accredited Spanish institution
  • Proof of accommodation in Spain (rental contract, student housing confirmation)
  • Private health insurance covering Spain for the full duration
  • Proof of payment of tuition fees (or first instalment)
  • All foreign documents may need a certified Spanish translation (sworn translator)

Application Process from Canada

Step-by-step guide to applying at the Spanish consulate in Canada.

1

Secure Your Place at a Spanish Institution

Obtain a formal acceptance or enrolment letter from your chosen school or university in Spain. This is the foundation document — you cannot apply without it.

2

Obtain Your RCMP Criminal Record Certificate

Apply for your RCMP Certified Criminal Record Check. Allow 2–4 weeks. Once received, submit it to Global Affairs Canada for apostille authentication.

3

Gather All Required Documents

Assemble all documents: passport, bank statements, health insurance, accommodation proof, medical certificate, tuition payment proof. Have any non-English/French documents translated by a certified Spanish translator.

4

Book Your Consulate Appointment

Contact the Spanish consulate with jurisdiction over your province. Appointments are booked online or by phone. Apply at least 3 months before your course start date — slots fill quickly in spring and summer.

5

Attend Your Appointment & Submit

Attend your consulate appointment in person with originals and copies of all documents. Pay the visa fee (approximately €60–100). You will receive a receipt and a timeline for decision.

6

Collect Your Visa & Travel

Processing typically takes 30–60 days. Once approved, collect your passport with the visa stamp. You must enter Spain within the validity window printed on the visa.

ℹ

Which consulate to use: Toronto covers Ontario and Eastern Canada. Montreal covers Quebec and the Maritimes. Ottawa (Embassy) covers federal-district residents. Calgary covers Alberta and the Prairies. Vancouver covers British Columbia. Always verify jurisdiction on the consulate's official website before booking.

Popular Study Destinations for Canadians

Where Canadian students in Spain typically choose to study.

Barcelona

Spain's most cosmopolitan city is the top choice for Canadians. Strong language school scene, major universities (UB, UPF, UAB), and an English-friendly expat community make settling in easier.

Seville

A favourite for those wanting full immersion in Spanish culture. World-class flamenco, a lively student population, and a strong language school scene. Lower cost of living than Barcelona.

Granada

Hugely popular for language study thanks to its university culture, affordable prices, and manageable size. The University of Granada has a long history of hosting international students.

Madrid & Valencia

Madrid suits those pursuing university degrees or business courses. Valencia is a growing destination — more affordable, excellent quality of life, and increasingly popular with North American students.

Work Rights During Your Studies

Understanding what Canadian student visa holders can and cannot do.

A Spanish student visa does not automatically include the right to work. However, once you are in Spain and have your student residence card (TIE), you can apply for a work authorisation as a student.

  • Authorised students may work up to 30 hours per week (some academic programmes cap this at 20 hours)
  • Work must not interfere with your studies
  • The work authorisation is separate from the student visa — you apply after arrival
  • Part-time roles, language teaching (conversation partners), and hospitality are common for students
  • Self-employment or freelance work is not permitted on a student visa
⚠

Do not rely on working to fund your stay. Financial proof at the visa application stage must show you can support yourself independently. Intended work income cannot substitute for proof of funds.

After Your Student Visa — What Comes Next?

Renewal, conversion, and pathways to longer-term residency.

A student visa can be renewed for as long as you remain enrolled in an accredited programme. Each renewal requires evidence of study progress (academic transcripts or attendance records) and updated financial proof.

The student visa does not lead directly to a work permit. However, there are established pathways:

  • Job Seeker Visa: After completing a degree at a Spanish university, graduates can apply for a 12-month job seeker visa to find employment
  • Highly Qualified Professional: If you secure a job offer, you can convert to a work permit directly
  • NIE & Residency: Time spent on a student visa does count towards long-term residency calculations under certain conditions

If you plan to stay in Spain long-term after your studies, book a consultation early to plan your route — the options depend on your specific situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Canadian citizens need a student visa for Spain?

Yes. Canadian citizens are non-EU nationals and require a Type D student visa for any study in Spain lasting more than 90 days. For shorter courses (under 90 days), Canadians can enter visa-free as tourists. The student visa must be obtained before leaving Canada — you cannot convert a tourist entry to a student visa once in Spain.

Which Spanish consulate do Canadians apply to?

Spain has consular offices in Toronto, Montreal, Ottawa, Calgary, and Vancouver. You must apply at the consulate with jurisdiction over your province. Toronto covers Ontario and Eastern Canada; Montreal covers Quebec; Calgary covers Alberta and the Prairies; Vancouver covers British Columbia. Check the consulate website to confirm which office serves your province.

What criminal record check do Canadians need?

Canadian applicants must obtain a Certified Criminal Record Check from the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP). This must then be apostilled by Global Affairs Canada. Since Canada joined the Hague Apostille Convention in 2024, the process is simpler — you submit the RCMP certificate directly to Global Affairs Canada for an apostille, which replaces the previous two-step authentication and legalisation process.

How much money do I need to show for a Spain student visa?

Spain requires approximately €600 per month, or around €7,200 for a full year. Canadian bank statements in CAD are accepted — the consulate will assess the equivalent. Provide 3–6 months of recent statements. Aim to show a consistent balance well above the minimum. Note that RRSP and locked-in accounts generally do not count as accessible funds.

How early should I apply before my course starts?

Apply at least 3 months before your course start date. The RCMP certificate takes 2–4 weeks, the Global Affairs Canada apostille takes 2–4 weeks, and consulate processing takes 30–60 days. Consulate appointments also fill up — especially from April through September. Starting the process early gives you a buffer for any delays.
