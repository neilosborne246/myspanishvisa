# MSV Content Gap Analysis — July 2026
*Competitors reviewed: Global Citizen Solutions, Lexidy, SpainExpat.com, Expatica*

---

## TIER 1 — HIGH PRIORITY GAPS
*Clear content gap + high search volume + competitor coverage confirmed*

### 1. Highly Qualified Professional Visa Spain (PAC / UGE-CE Visa)
**Slug:** `blog-highly-qualified-professional-visa-spain`
Both GCS and Lexidy list this as a primary service. It's a full visa route under Ley 14/2013 for senior employees, managers, and specialists relocated by companies to Spain. Minimum salary threshold is ~€40k–€54k (2026). We have zero coverage. High commercial intent from corporate HR teams and professionals being transferred.

### 2. Spanish Citizenship by Descent (Ley de Nietos / Grandchildren's Law)
**Slug:** `blog-spanish-citizenship-by-descent`
The Democratic Memory Law (2022–2024 extension) allowed grandchildren of Spanish exiles to claim citizenship without residency. Enormous search volume especially from Latin America. We have citizenship by naturalisation, dual nationality, and citizenship timeline articles — but nothing on citizenship by descent. This is arguably the biggest single gap.

### 3. Spain Entrepreneur Visa / Startup Visa Guide
**Slug:** `blog-spain-entrepreneur-visa-startup-visa`
Distinct from `blog-start-business-spain.html` (which covers the practicalities of opening a business). The Entrepreneur Visa is a specific immigration route under Law 14/2013 for non-EU founders with business plans of economic interest to Spain. Requires ENISA/DGPYME endorsement. Competitors (Lexidy, Vissumlex, Jobbatical) cover this well. We have nothing.

### 4. Living in Seville: Expat Guide
**Slug:** `blog-living-in-seville-expat-guide`
Spain's 4th-largest city and a major expat/retiree destination — we cover 10 other cities/regions but have no Seville guide. Competitors do. High search volume for "living in Seville expat", "moving to Seville guide", "Seville expat community."

### 5. Spanish Inheritance Tax for Expats
**Slug:** `blog-spain-inheritance-tax-expats`
A major financial concern for anyone buying property or retiring in Spain. We have wealth tax, income tax, property tax, and double taxation articles — but no inheritance/succession tax guide. Competitors cover this. High intent from retirees and property buyers.

### 6. Moving to Spain from Germany
**Slug:** `blog-moving-to-spain-from-germany`
Germans are one of the largest expat groups in Spain (second only to UK/Romanian). We cover UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Ireland, South Africa, New Zealand — but not Germany, despite it being the biggest EU source market for Spanish expats.

## TIER 2 — MEDIUM PRIORITY
*Gap confirmed; strong search volume; some adjacent content exists*

### 7. Spanish Passport: Visa-Free Countries & Passport Strength
**Slug:** `blog-spanish-passport-visa-free-countries`
GCS just published "Spanish Passport Visa-Free Countries 2026" and a full Spanish Passport guide. For people going through the citizenship journey, this is a key "what do I get?" article. We have no passport coverage.

### 8. Capital Gains Tax Spain for Expats (Property & Investments)
**Slug:** `blog-capital-gains-tax-spain-expats`
We cover income tax, wealth tax, Beckham Law, and double taxation — but not CGT specifically. Crucial for anyone selling a Spanish property or with investment income. Includes the special 3% non-resident retention rule (a major practical concern for non-resident sellers).

### 9. Spain Work Permit for Employed Workers (Permiso de Trabajo)
**Slug:** `blog-spain-work-permit-employed-workers`
We have excellent content on the DNV, NLV, student visa, and various conversion routes — but no standalone guide on the standard work permit for people moving to Spain with a job offer. This covers the quota work visa, the employer authorisation process, and the UGE-CE route.

### 10. Living in Marbella: Expat Guide
**Slug:** `blog-living-in-marbella-expat-guide`
One of the highest-search expat destinations on the Costa del Sol. We have Costa del Sol generally but Marbella gets its own high-volume searches. Strong retirement and HNW audience.

### 11. Moving to Spain from the Netherlands
**Slug:** `blog-moving-to-spain-from-netherlands`
The Dutch are among the top 5 expat nationalities in Spain. EU citizens, so a simpler visa process — but still lots of practical questions about registration, taxes, healthcare. No coverage currently.

### 12. Selling Property in Spain as a Non-Resident (3% Retention Rule)
**Slug:** `blog-selling-property-spain-non-resident`
When non-residents sell Spanish property, 3% of the sale price is withheld by the buyer and paid to Hacienda as a tax deposit. This is widely misunderstood and a major pain point. We have buying guides but nothing on selling.

### 13. New Build vs Resale Property in Spain
**Slug:** `blog-new-build-vs-resale-property-spain`
A key decision for property buyers — different taxes (IVA vs ITP), different risks, different timelines. Competitors cover this as part of their property content. We have pitfalls of buying but not this comparison.

### 14. Spain Arras Contract Explained
**Slug:** `blog-spain-arras-contract-explained`
The penal deposit contract signed before a property sale (typically 10%). Buyer loses it if they pull out; seller pays double if they pull out. Not widely understood by foreign buyers. Lexidy offers it as a service. We have property legal guides but not this specific topic.

### 15. Pros and Cons of Living in Spain (Honest Expat Guide)
**Slug:** `blog-pros-cons-living-in-spain-expats`
We have `blog-is-spain-good-place-retire-pros-cons.html` (retirement-focused) but nothing for the general expat audience. A "pros and cons" article ranks well and converts well as a top-of-funnel piece for people at the research stage.

### 16. Moving to Spain from India
**Slug:** `blog-moving-to-spain-from-india`
We have `student-visa-spain-india.html` as a landing page, but no comprehensive "moving to Spain from India" blog. Large and growing audience — India is a top-5 applicant nationality for EU visas. Strong interest in both DNV and NLV routes.

## TIER 3 — NICE TO HAVE
*Lower urgency but genuine gaps vs competitors*

### 17. Living in Ibiza: Expat Guide
**Slug:** `blog-living-in-ibiza-expat-guide`
Popular with digital nomads, creatives, and wealthy expats. Distinct from Mallorca in character and audience.

### 18. Moving to Spain from France
**Slug:** `blog-moving-to-spain-from-france`
We compare Spain vs France for retirees but have no guide for French people moving to Spain. EU citizens so simpler process, but still practical demand.

### 19. Moving to Spain from Brazil / Argentina (Ibero-American Citizenship Route)
**Slug:** `blog-moving-to-spain-from-brazil` / `blog-moving-to-spain-from-argentina`
Ibero-American citizens qualify for Spanish citizenship after just 2 years residency (vs 10 for others). Huge search volume from both countries. Also relevant for `blog-spanish-citizenship-by-descent` (many descendants of Spanish emigrants).

### 20. Spanish Community Fees (Gastos de Comunidad) Explained
**Slug:** `blog-spain-community-fees-gastos-comunidad`
Property owners in apartment blocks pay monthly community fees — a frequently confusing topic for foreign buyers. We cover horizontal property law and property taxes but not this specific recurring cost.

### 21. Spain Car Insurance for Expats
**Slug:** `blog-car-insurance-spain-expats`
We have the driving licence exchange guide and registering a car, but not car insurance. Practical and searchable.

### 22. Spain vs Greece for Expats / Retirees
**Slug:** `blog-spain-vs-greece-expats-retirees`
We compare Spain vs Portugal, Spain vs France, Spain vs Italy — but not Greece, which now has its own retirement visa (FIP) and digital nomad visa and is a growing competitor destination.

### 23. Spain Volunteer Visa / Volunteering in Spain
**Slug:** `blog-volunteering-spain-visa-foreigners`
A niche but underserved audience. Spain has a specific "Volunteering Visa" and many people want to spend time volunteering without a work permit.

### 24. Spanish Food Culture Guide for Expats
**Slug:** `blog-spanish-food-culture-guide-expats`
We have `blog-spain-culture-shock-what-to-expect.html` which likely touches food, but a dedicated food culture guide (mealtimes, tipping, supermarkets, markets) is a high-traffic lifestyle piece that Expatica features prominently.

### 25. Spain Salary / Tax Calculator (Interactive Tool)
Not a blog post — Expatica has a "Spain Income Tax Calculator" tool which drives significant traffic and return visits. Worth considering as a static HTML calculator page.
