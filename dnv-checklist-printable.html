The Complete Spain Digital Nomad Visa Checklist 2026

Every document required for employed and self-employed DNV applicants — with post-approval actions

Updated: April 2026 11 Sections Employed + Self-Employed Routes Includes Beckham Law guide

How to use this checklist: This checklist covers both employed applicants EMPLOYEE and self-employed/freelance applicants SELF-EMPLOYED. Items marked with both apply to everyone. The DNV is processed via the UGE (Unidad de Grandes Empresas) in Madrid — not at a consulate for in-Spain applications. For applications from abroad, apply at your home country's Spanish consulate. Pay close attention to APOSTILLE and TRANSLATE tags.

Criminal Record Certificate

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Criminal record from country of nationality REQUIRED
Valid for 3 months. UK: ACRO. USA: FBI. Canada: RCMP. Australia: AFP.
Apostille on criminal record certificate APOSTILLE
Hague Apostille required for all non-Spanish documents.
Sworn Spanish translation of criminal record TRANSLATE
By certified sworn translator (traductor jurado). Machine translations not accepted.
Criminal record from country of residence (if different) APOSTILLE TRANSLATE
Required if resident in a different country in the past 5 years.

Employment Income Evidence — Employed Applicants

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Employer letter confirming remote work authorisation EMPLOYEE REQUIRED
On company headed paper, signed by HR/director. Must confirm: employee name, role, salary, remote working permission, that >80% of work is for non-Spanish clients.
Letter confirms company is established for at least 1 year
The employing company must have been operating for at least 12 months.
Letter confirms employee has been employed for 3+ months
Minimum 3-month employment relationship required.
Sworn Spanish translation of employer letter TRANSLATE
Required for all documents not in Spanish.
Employment contract — original or certified copy EMPLOYEE
Must show salary, role, and remote working clause. With sworn translation.
Recent payslips (3–6 months) showing income level EMPLOYEE
Must show gross salary meeting income threshold (200% IPREM = approx. €2,762/month in 2026).
Bank statements confirming salary payments received
Corroborates payslips. Last 3–6 months.
Company registration documents of employer
Evidence the company is legitimately registered. Companies House extract (UK), incorporation certificate (US), etc.

Employer letter prompt: Your employer letter should state on headed paper: employee full name, passport number, job title, annual/monthly salary, date of employment start, confirmation of remote working authorisation, confirmation that >80% of activity is for companies not registered in Spain, and confirmation the company has been active for at least 12 months.

Pre-Application Timeline

Timeframe Action Notes
3 months out Order criminal record certificate (allow 4–12 weeks for FBI apostille) Start this first — FBI apostille is the longest to obtain.
3 months out Brief employer / start preparing employer letter Give employer enough time to prepare a comprehensive letter.
3 months out Purchase Spanish private health insurance Policy must be active before application. Confirm no-copay compliance in writing.
6 weeks out Obtain medical certificate and arrange apostille Valid for only 3 months — do not order too early.
6 weeks out Commission sworn translations of all non-Spanish documents Allow 1–2 weeks. Ensure translator is certified (traductor jurado).
6 weeks out Collate company registration documents, contracts, invoices, bank statements Ensure all income documents are within required date range.
2 weeks out Complete Form EX-21 / consulate application form Must be in Spanish. One per applicant.
2 weeks out Final checklist review — confirm all apostilles and translations are attached Lay out every document with its apostille and translation together.
Day before Prepare originals + photocopies (2 copies of each document) Most processing offices require originals plus copies.
11

Post-Approval Checklist (after DNV is granted)

✓
Task
Deadline / Notes
Register padrón (empadronamiento) at local Ayuntamiento ASAP
Required before TIE appointment. Bring passport, rental contract, and completed census form.
Apply for TIE card at Oficina de Extranjería WITHIN 30 DAYS
Book via extranjeria.ses.mir.es. Need EX-17 form, Modelo 790 fee, passport, padrón, photos.
Register with Spanish Social Security (Seguridad Social)
Required to work legally in Spain. Obtain your social security number (número de afiliación). This triggers the Beckham Law deadline.
Apply for Beckham Law (Régimen Especial de Impatriados) WITHIN 6 MONTHS
6 months from first social security registration. File Modelo 149 with Agencia Tributaria. 24% flat tax rate on Spanish-source income for up to 6 years.
Register as autónomo (if self-employed in Spain)
Self-employed workers must register as autónomo. Monthly social security contribution approx. €300–400. Confirm with a Spanish gestor.
Obtain digital certificate (certificado digital) via FNMT
Apply at sede.fnmt.gob.es after TIE is issued. Essential for online dealings with Hacienda and Seguridad Social.
Open Spanish bank account
Needed for Spanish salary payments, tax payments, and direct debits.
Confirm Beckham Law election with Hacienda
Follow up to confirm your Modelo 149 has been accepted and your tax status updated.

Beckham Law deadline warning: The 6-month window to apply for the Beckham Law tax regime runs from your first registration with Spanish social security — not from your arrival date or visa grant date. Missing this deadline permanently forecloses this benefit. If unsure, consult a Spanish tax advisor (asesor fiscal) immediately after registering.
