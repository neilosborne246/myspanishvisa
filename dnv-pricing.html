Additional Costs to Know About

We include everything we can in the service fee. Here's what sits outside it — and why.

🏥
Private Health Insurance

Required for employed applicants. Self-employed (autónomo) applicants are covered by Spain's public health system. For employed applicants, arrange a DNV-compliant policy via spanish-healthinsurance.com.

🏛️
Government Tasa

UGE applications (in Spain): the tasa is included in our fee. Consulate applications (from abroad): the tasa is payable directly to Spanish authorities at submission. We confirm the exact amount at document preparation stage.

📋
Apostille & Criminal Record Fees

You'll need apostilled criminal record certificates from every country you've lived in over the last five years. The cost varies by country and is payable directly to your home government — we'll guide you through exactly what to get.

⚖️
Beckham Law Tax Registration

DNV holders can opt into Spain's special 24% flat tax regime for up to 6 years. This is a separate post-approval service handled by our specialist tax team — not bundled into the DNV fee.

🪪
TIE Residence Card Appointment

Once approved and in Spain, you'll book your TIE appointment directly at your local police station within 30 days. There's a small government fee for the card itself. We walk you through the whole process.

📄
Sworn Translations — What's Included

Translations are included up to €200 (new) / €100 (renewal). This covers criminal record, proof of income, and employer/client letter. Any excess is charged at cost and confirmed with you in advance.
