Frequently Asked Questions

You can get by in major cities and expat areas without fluent Spanish, especially in areas like Costa del Sol, Barcelona, and Madrid. However, Spanish will make everyday life significantly easier and is required for some bureaucratic processes.

Spain has an excellent public healthcare system, consistently ranked among the best in Europe. As a resident, you may be entitled to access public health once registered on the social security system. Private health insurance is required for most visa applications.

Spain is generally more affordable than northern Europe for everyday costs — food, eating out, and utilities tend to be lower. However, rental costs in Madrid and Barcelona have risen significantly. Overall, many expats find Spain more affordable than the UK or USA.
