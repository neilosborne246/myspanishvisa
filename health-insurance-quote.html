What You’ll Be Asked

  • Who is to be covered, and each person’s date of birth
  • Where in Spain you live or plan to live
  • Your preferred start date
  • Your visa route, if you have one
  • Any questions or special requirements

Before cover is confirmed, each person completes a health questionnaire.

What Happens Next

  1. Your personalised quote. Spanish Health Insurance prepares it from the details you give.
  2. Health questionnaire and acceptance. Sanitas reviews each person’s answers.
  3. Payment. Payment terms depend on the plan; some visa policies must be paid annually upfront. Your quote states the terms.
  4. Your documents. Once your application is accepted and paid, your policy documents are issued, and any certificate you need can show a future start date agreed with you.
