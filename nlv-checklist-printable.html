The Complete Non-Lucrative Visa Checklist 2026

Every document, translation, and certification required for a successful NLV application

Updated: April 2026 10 Sections Printable Format For use at Spanish consulates worldwide

How to use this checklist: Work through each section in order, ticking off items as you gather them. Pay close attention to APOSTILLE and TRANSLATE tags — many applicants are refused because apostilles or translations are missing. Items marked DEADLINE have time-sensitive requirements. Consulate requirements can vary — always confirm with your specific consulate before applying.

1

Identity Documents

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Valid passport — original + photocopy of all pages REQUIRED
Must be valid for at least 1 year beyond visa expiry. Copy all pages including blank pages.
Biometric passport photos × 2
Recent (within 6 months), white background, 35×45mm. Check consulate specific requirements.
Previous Spanish visa or residence permits (if applicable)
Include copies of any previous Spain visas held.
National ID card (some consulates — check requirements)
Some consulates accept national ID alongside passport. Verify locally.
2

Criminal Record Certificate

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Criminal record certificate from country of nationality REQUIRED
UK: ACRO Police Certificate. USA: FBI Apostille. Canada: RCMP. Australia: AFP. Must be issued within 3 months of application.
Apostille on criminal record certificate APOSTILLE
Hague Apostille required. In the UK via FCDO. In the US via your State Secretary of State or FBI directly.
Sworn Spanish translation of criminal record TRANSLATE
Must be by a certified sworn translator (traductor jurado). Do not use machine translation.
Criminal record from country of residence (if different from nationality)
Required if you have lived outside your home country in the past 5 years. Same apostille + translation rules apply.
Apostille + sworn translation for country of residence certificate APOSTILLE TRANSLATE
Same requirements as above.

Criminal record certificates are typically valid for only 3 months from the date of issue. Do not order yours too early or it may expire before your appointment.

3

Medical Certificate

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Medical certificate confirming no contagious diseases REQUIRED
Must be on headed paper, signed by a licensed physician. Valid for 3 months. Ask your GP or a private doctor.
Apostille on medical certificate APOSTILLE
Required by most consulates. Check if your specific consulate requires it — some accept a notarised copy instead.
Sworn Spanish translation of medical certificate TRANSLATE
Must be by certified sworn translator.

Tip: Request a medical certificate on a specific Spanish template if your consulate provides one. Some consulates in the US, UK, and Australia publish recommended medical certificate formats on their websites.

4

Financial Evidence

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Bank statements — last 3–6 months REQUIRED
Show sufficient passive income. Minimum approx. €2,400/month (400% IPREM 2026) for single applicant.
Pension income evidence (if applicable)
Annual pension statement or letter from pension provider. UK: State Pension forecast letter or P60. US: Social Security award letter.
Investment income evidence (if applicable)
Investment statements, dividend records, brokerage account statements showing regular passive income.
Rental income evidence (if applicable)
Tenancy agreements + bank statements showing rental payments received. Include property ownership documents.
Savings evidence (lump sum — some consulates accept this)
Bank savings statement showing sufficient lump sum (typically €28,800+ for single applicant). Confirm with your specific consulate.
Sworn Spanish translation of financial documents (if required) TRANSLATE
Some consulates require translations; others accept English originals. Verify with your consulate.
5

Health Insurance

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Private health insurance certificate for Spain REQUIRED
Must be issued by a company authorised to operate in Spain. No copayments (no copay/no deductible). Full coverage throughout Spain.
Policy covers all of Spain (not travel insurance)
Travel insurance is NOT acceptable. Must be a Spanish health insurance policy.
No copayments / no deductibles confirmed in certificate
The certificate must explicitly state "sin copago" or equivalent. Some policies require an additional letter confirming this.
Policy valid for full duration of visa (minimum 1 year)
Policy must not expire before visa end date.
Covers all family members on application
Each applicant on the form needs their own insurance certificate or must be named on the family policy.
Insurance certificate in Spanish or with Spanish translation
Some consulates require the certificate in Spanish. Check with your insurer — many Spanish providers issue bilingual certificates.
6

Spain Accommodation Proof

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Rental contract for accommodation in Spain REQUIRED
Must show address, start/end dates, landlord and tenant names. Minimum 1-year contract preferred.
Property title deed (if property owner in Spain)
Escritura de compraventa. Include a simple translation if not in Spanish already.
Sworn Spanish translation of rental contract (if in English) TRANSLATE
Most consulates require the rental contract in Spanish or with sworn translation.
7

Application Form and Fees

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Form EX-01 — completed and signed REQUIRED
Download from sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es. Complete in Spanish. One form per applicant.
Visa fee payment confirmation / receipt
Fees vary by consulate and nationality. Check current fee at your consulate website. Usually paid at appointment.
Consulate appointment confirmation
Print confirmation of your cita previa (appointment booking). Bring to the appointment.
8

Family Member Documents (if applicable)

✓
Document
Notes / Requirements
Marriage certificate (for spouse) APOSTILLE TRANSLATE
Original + apostille + sworn Spanish translation.
Birth certificates for children APOSTILLE TRANSLATE
Each child's birth certificate with apostille and sworn Spanish translation.
Criminal record for spouse and dependants over 16 APOSTILLE TRANSLATE
Each adult family member needs their own criminal record check, apostille, and translation.
Medical certificates for all family members APOSTILLE TRANSLATE
Each family member must provide their own medical certificate.
Increased financial evidence for family members
Add 75% IPREM per additional adult and 25% IPREM per child to the base income requirement.
Health insurance covering all family members
Each family member must be covered by Spanish private health insurance.
9

Pre-Application Timeline

Timeframe Action Notes
3 months out Order criminal record certificate (FBI/ACRO/RCMP etc.) — allow 4–12 weeks for FBI apostille FBI background checks are the longest to obtain. Order first.
3 months out Research and purchase compliant Spanish health insurance Policy must be active before application. Allow time to confirm no-copay compliance.
3 months out Secure Spain accommodation and obtain signed rental contract Many consulates want to see a contract in place before applying.
6 weeks out Obtain medical certificate and arrange apostille Valid for 3 months — do not order too early.
6 weeks out Commission sworn translations of criminal record, medical certificate, financial documents Allow 1–2 weeks for sworn translation turnaround.
6 weeks out Book consulate appointment (cita previa) Appointments can be scarce — book as early as your consulate allows.
2 weeks out Complete and print Form EX-01 One per applicant. Must be completed in full.
2 weeks out Collate all documents and check expiry dates Criminal record and medical cert must be within 3 months of appointment date.
Day before Final document check against this checklist Ensure all apostilles and translations are attached to originals.
Day before Prepare originals + photocopies of every document Most consulates want both original and copies. Bring at least 2 copies of each.
10

Post-Arrival Checklist (after entering Spain on your NLV)

✓
Task
Deadline / Notes
Register padrón (empadronamiento) at local Ayuntamiento ASAP
Needed before TIE appointment. Bring passport, rental contract, and completed form.
Apply for TIE card at Oficina de Extranjería WITHIN 30 DAYS
Book cita previa at extranjeria.ses.mir.es. Bring EX-17 form, fee payment (Modelo 790), passport, padrón, and photos.
Confirm NIE number and collect TIE card
TIE collection is a separate appointment. Bring receipt from original appointment.
Open Spanish bank account
Bring TIE/NIE, passport, padrón certificate, and proof of income. Recommended: Sabadell, BBVA, or CaixaBank for expats.
Register with local GP (médico de cabecera)
If your health insurance provides access to a GP network, register at your nearest clinic. Bring TIE and padrón.
Obtain digital certificate (certificado digital) via FNMT
Required for online dealings with Spanish authorities. Apply at sede.fnmt.gob.es after receiving TIE card.
