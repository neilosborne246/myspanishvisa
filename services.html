What's Included in Full Application Management

A detailed breakdown of every step we take on your behalf — from your initial assessment to your first year settled in Spain.

1

Initial Assessment

We review your full situation — income, nationality, family, timeline — and confirm the optimal visa route for your case. If there are complications, we identify them early.

2

Personalised Document Checklist

You receive a precise list of every document your application requires — apostille requirements, translation needs, and consulate-specific formatting requirements included.

3

Source Document Review

Before you get anything translated or apostilled, we review your source documents to confirm they will meet consulate requirements. We flag and fix problems before they cause delays.

4

Translation Coordination

We advise on exactly which documents need certified Spanish translation and connect you with qualified translators. We review translations for accuracy before they go into your application.

5

Application Package Preparation

We prepare every official form, assemble your complete application package, and conduct a final review against current consulate requirements before submission.

6

Consulate Submission Support

We brief you fully on your consulate appointment — what to bring, what questions to expect, and how to present your application. You walk in prepared.

7

Approval Tracking

We monitor the status of your application and keep you updated. If the consulate requires additional information, we respond immediately.

8

TIE Card Guidance & Post-Arrival Support

On arrival in Spain, we guide you through registering at the padron (municipal register), booking your TIE card appointment, and opening a Spanish bank account.

9

12-Month Renewal Reminder

We automatically alert you 90 days before your visa expires. Renewal is a separate service — but you'll never miss the window with our reminder system in place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how our Spain visa service works.

How is My Spanish Visa different from a law firm?

My Spanish Visa is a specialist Spain visa service platform — not a general law firm. We focus exclusively on Spain residency visas for English-speaking non-EU nationals, with a fully online process and English-language communication throughout. We are backed by PLS Platinum Legal Spain, so every case has qualified Spanish immigration lawyer oversight — but the client-facing experience is built for simplicity and clarity, not legal jargon.

Do I need to be in the UK or US to use My Spanish Visa?

No. Our service is fully online and available from any country. You manage your entire case through our secure client portal — document uploads, case tracking, and messaging with your case manager. The only in-person step is attending your local Spanish consulate appointment, which we prepare you for in full. Most of our clients are in the UK, US, Australia, Canada, and Ireland, but we work with applicants worldwide.

What if my Spain visa application is rejected?

We conduct a thorough pre-submission review of every application — this significantly reduces the risk of rejection. Common rejection causes (insufficient financial evidence, incorrect health insurance, missing apostilles) are caught before we submit. If a rejection does occur despite our review, we analyse the decision, advise on appeal or reapplication options, and support you through the next steps — at no additional service charge for Full Application Management clients.

Can you help with Spain visa renewals?

Yes. We manage Non-Lucrative Visa renewals, Digital Nomad Visa renewals, and Student Visa renewals. Full Application Management clients receive an automatic renewal reminder 90 days before their visa expires, plus a discounted renewal service. If you used us for your initial application, your case file is already on record — making renewals faster and more straightforward.

Is the payment secure?

Yes. All payments are processed through secure, encrypted payment infrastructure. For Full Application Management, payment is structured across 3 milestones — which means you only pay in full once your application package is prepared and ready to submit. No recurring charges, no surprise fees. All pricing is confirmed in writing before you commit to any paid service.
