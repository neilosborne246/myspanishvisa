Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the quiz and Spain visa eligibility.

How does the Spain visa eligibility quiz work?

Our quiz asks six key questions about your nationality, purpose for moving, employment status, financial situation, timeline, and family composition. Based on your answers, the tool instantly recommends the visa type most suited to your circumstances.

Is this quiz accurate for my visa decision?

The quiz provides personalised recommendations based on your answers, but it's not a legal assessment. Visa eligibility depends on specific circumstances and can change with immigration law updates. We recommend consulting with a qualified immigration lawyer or booking a consultation for definitive guidance.

Can EU citizens get a visa to live in Spain?

No. EU/EEA citizens don't need a visa to live in Spain. You have freedom of movement rights and can move to Spain without a visa. You will need to register with local authorities once you arrive.

What is the Non-Lucrative Visa and who can apply?

The Non-Lucrative Visa (Visa de No Lucrativa) is designed for people with passive income (pensions, investments, property rental). You typically need to prove monthly income of €1,260+, have private health insurance, and show no intention to work in Spain.

What is the Digital Nomad Visa for Spain?

The Digital Nomad Visa (Visa de Nómada Digital) lets remote workers live in Spain while employed by non-Spanish companies. It requires proof of income (usually €2,300+/month), a valid employment contract, and private health insurance.

How long does it take to get a Spain visa?

Processing times vary by visa type and consulate. Most take 15–40 business days from submission. Some consulates may take longer during peak periods. We recommend starting the process at least 3–4 months before your planned move date.

Can I work in Spain on a Non-Lucrative Visa?

The Non-Lucrative Visa restricts employment in Spain. You cannot work for a Spanish employer or be self-employed in Spain. However, remote work for a non-Spanish company may be permitted in some cases. For clarification, consult an immigration lawyer.

What should I do after getting my visa recommendation?

After receiving your recommendation, we suggest: (1) reviewing the full visa guide, (2) gathering required documents, (3) booking a consultation with our team to discuss your specific situation, and (4) submitting your application through the appropriate Spanish consulate.
