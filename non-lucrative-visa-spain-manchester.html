Northern England Applicants: No London Trip Needed

If you live in the North of England, your Spain NLV application goes through BLS Manchester — not London.

One of the most common questions from Northern England applicants is whether they need to travel to London to apply for the Spanish Non-Lucrative Visa. The answer is no. BLS International operates a dedicated visa application centre in Manchester that handles Spanish visa applications for the entire Northern England region and Scotland.

The Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV) is Spain's most popular long-stay visa for UK nationals who want to live in Spain without working — retirees, those with passive investment income, and lifestyle movers. Since Brexit, UK citizens apply as third-country nationals and must go through BLS International, the authorised visa processing centre contracted by the Spanish government.

✓

Northern England advantage: BLS Manchester typically has shorter appointment wait times and faster processing than BLS London due to lower application volumes. If you are planning your move to Spain around a specific date, Manchester applicants often have more predictable timelines.

Who should use BLS Manchester:

  • Greater Manchester residents (Manchester, Salford, Stockport, Bolton, Oldham)
  • West Yorkshire (Leeds, Bradford, Huddersfield, Wakefield, Halifax)
  • South Yorkshire (Sheffield, Rotherham, Doncaster, Barnsley)
  • Merseyside (Liverpool, Birkenhead, Southport)
  • Tyne and Wear / North East (Newcastle, Sunderland, Gateshead, Durham)
  • Lancashire (Preston, Blackpool, Burnley, Lancaster)
  • Cumbria, Cheshire, and wider North of England
  • Scotland (many Scottish applicants also use BLS Manchester — see BLS website for your postcode)

Step-by-Step NLV Application from Manchester

Eight steps from eligibility check to entering Spain on your new visa.

1

Check Your Eligibility

The NLV requires passive income of approximately €2,400/month (single) or €3,000+/month (couple), no right to work in Spain, and no disqualifying criminal record. Use our free eligibility check to confirm you qualify before gathering documents.

2

Gather Your Documents

Collect all required documents: UK passport, 3–6 months of bank statements, pension letters from DWP or private providers, health insurance certificate, ACRO criminal record, medical certificate, proof of accommodation in Spain, and the EX-01 application form. See the complete checklist below.

3

Apostille UK Documents via FCDO

Send all UK public documents to the FCDO Legalisation Office in Milton Keynes for apostille certification. Cost: £75 per document, standard 4–8 week turnaround. Batch all documents together to save time. This step is required before any document can be submitted as part of your Spanish visa application.

4

Apply for ACRO Criminal Record Certificate

Apply immediately at acro.police.uk — £45 standard (10–15 working days), £98 fast-track (3–5 days). Once received, send to FCDO for apostille (batch with step 3), then to a sworn Spanish translator. Start this as early as possible — it typically takes 6–10 weeks end to end and cannot be rushed without paying the fast-track fee at each stage.

5

Get Sworn Spanish Translations

After apostille, every document must be translated into Spanish by a sworn translator (traductor jurado) officially recognised by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Cost: €50–€150 per document, typically 1–2 weeks. Find sworn translators via the Spanish consulate website or Ministry of Foreign Affairs directory. Online translation tools or unofficial translators are not accepted.

6

Purchase Spanish-Authorised Health Insurance

Buy private health insurance from a provider authorised in Spain. The policy must cover you comprehensively in Spain with no copay clause and a minimum 1-year term. UK NHS, EHIC, or travel insurance are not accepted. Annual cost: approximately €700–€2,500 depending on age. Obtain a written certificate of coverage to include in your dossier.

7

Book and Attend BLS Manchester Appointment

Book your BLS Manchester appointment online once your dossier is nearly complete. Attend in person with all original documents plus copies, pay the consulate fee (€80–€150), and receive a tracking reference. BLS staff will check completeness before accepting your dossier.

8

Wait for the Decision, Then Travel to Spain

Allow 6–12 weeks for the consulate decision. Once approved, collect your stamped passport from BLS Manchester and enter Spain within the validity window (typically 3 months). Upon arrival, register at the Extranjería (foreigners' office) within 30 days for your NIE and TIE residency card.

Document Checklist for Manchester Applicants

The same requirements apply whether you use BLS Manchester or BLS London. Here is what you need, with UK-specific notes.

✓ Valid passport — minimum 6 months validity remaining. Bring the original plus certified copies of all pages.
✓ Completed EX-01 application form — the official Spanish national visa application form, printed, completed, and signed.
✓ 2 passport photographs — recent, white background, to Spanish consulate specifications.
✓ ACRO criminal record certificate — apostilled by FCDO + sworn Spanish translation. Apply at acro.police.uk. Not a DBS check.
✓ Medical certificate — signed letter from your GP or a private clinic confirming you are free from contagious diseases, translated into Spanish by a sworn translator.
✓ Proof of income — 3–6 months of UK bank statements, DWP pension letter, private pension statements, or investment income proof. Apostilled and translated.
✓ Private health insurance certificate — from a Spanish-authorised provider, comprehensive coverage in Spain, no copay clause, minimum 1-year term.
✓ Proof of accommodation in Spain — rental contract for Spanish property or title deeds (escritura) if you are buying.
✓ Application fee — consulate fee of €80–€150, payable at BLS Manchester on the day. Non-refundable regardless of outcome.
⚠️

Apostille and translation are mandatory. Every UK document must be apostilled by the FCDO (£75 per document, 4–8 weeks) and then translated by a sworn Spanish translator. Documents in English are not accepted without both steps completed. See our full document checklist guide for details on each document.

Income Requirements 2026

The NLV income thresholds are set by the Spanish government and are the same for Manchester and London applicants.

2026 NLV Income Thresholds (IPREM-based)

Single applicant ~€2,400/month
Couple (two applicants) ~€3,000–€3,100/month combined
Each additional dependent +~€600/month per person
Savings alternative (12 months) €28,800–€38,000 liquid

Northern England applicants typically present income from UK State Pension (DWP letters), private or occupational pension payments from providers such as Teachers' Pension, NHS Pension, or company schemes, investment income, rental income from Northern England properties, and UK savings accounts. All of these are accepted sources.

The key requirement is that the income is passive (you do not need to work for it) and consistent. Spanish consulates want to see 3–6 months of bank statements showing regular deposits — for example, monthly pension payments arriving consistently from the DWP. A single month's statement is not sufficient.

For a complete breakdown of income types, how to calculate totals, and how to present UK financial documents to the consulate, see our NLV income requirements guide.

Processing Times from Manchester

What Manchester applicants can realistically expect — and why it can be faster than London.

From the date of your BLS Manchester appointment, the typical processing time is 6–12 weeks. Manchester generally processes faster than London because of lower application volume. During peak seasons (May to August), allow up to 12 weeks. Outside peak periods, 6–8 weeks is achievable.

What affects your Manchester processing time:

  • Dossier completeness: An incomplete or incorrectly prepared dossier causes the consulate to request additional information, adding weeks to the timeline.
  • Time of year: Peak summer applications (May–August) increase processing times across all UK centres.
  • Spanish consulate destination: Which region of Spain you are moving to determines which Spanish consulate reviews your application — some are faster than others.
  • Translation quality: Poorly formatted or unclear translations sometimes prompt clarification requests from the consulate.
✓

Full timeline from start to arrival in Spain: Budget 4–5 months total from the decision to begin the process to arriving in Spain. This covers: ACRO application (2–3 weeks), FCDO apostille (4–8 weeks), sworn translations (1–2 weeks), BLS appointment (book 2–4 weeks ahead), consulate processing (6–12 weeks), and travel preparation. Manchester applicants often complete this slightly faster than London applicants.

Common Mistakes Northern England Applicants Make

These errors account for most delays and rejections at BLS Manchester.

Confusing ACRO with DBS

The mistake: Applying for a DBS check (used for UK employment vetting) instead of the ACRO Standard Disclosure (Police Certificate), which is the internationally recognised criminal record certificate required for Spanish visas.

Fix: Apply at acro.police.uk specifically for the ACRO Standard Disclosure. It costs £45 standard or £98 fast-track. Once received, apostille it and have it translated by a sworn Spanish translator.

Using UK travel insurance instead of NLV-compliant health cover

The mistake: Purchasing a standard UK annual travel policy or relying on an NHS EHIC/GHIC card as the health insurance requirement. Neither satisfies the NLV health insurance requirement.

Fix: Buy a specific private health insurance policy from a Spanish-authorised insurer. The policy must cover you comprehensively in Spain with no copay clause. See our health insurance guide for qualifying providers.

Not apostilling bank statements and pension letters

The mistake: Submitting UK financial documents directly in English, assuming they will be accepted because they are from official UK institutions. Spanish consulates require apostille plus sworn translation, without exception.

Fix: Send all financial documents (bank statements, DWP letters, private pension statements) to the FCDO Legalisation Office (£75 each, 4–8 weeks). Then commission sworn Spanish translations before your BLS appointment.

Submitting outdated bank statements

The mistake: Bank statements that are more than 3 months old by the time they reach BLS Manchester. This is a common timing error when apostille or translation takes longer than expected.

Fix: Plan your document timeline carefully. Factor in FCDO apostille time (4–8 weeks) and translation time (1–2 weeks). You may need to obtain fresh statements if the timeline slips and existing ones expire.

Not confirming accommodation in Spain before applying

The mistake: Submitting an application without proof of where you will live in Spain. Many Northern England applicants plan to search for property after the visa is approved — but proof of accommodation is required at the application stage.

Fix: Secure a rental contract for your Spanish property before applying. Remote rental agreements with Spanish landlords are common for this purpose. Alternatively, if purchasing, title deeds must be finalised before submitting the application.

Providing only one month of bank statements

The mistake: Including only the most recent month's statement instead of the 3–6 months that consulates want to see. A single statement doesn't demonstrate income consistency.

Fix: Submit 3–6 consecutive months of statements. The consulate is looking for reliable, recurring passive income deposits — not just a single snapshot. Regular pension payments visible month-after-month make the strongest case.

Frequently Asked Questions — Manchester NLV Applicants

Do I have to go to London to apply for a Spain NLV from Manchester?

No. If you live in the North of England, you apply through BLS International Manchester. BLS Manchester handles Spanish visa applications for residents of Greater Manchester, Leeds, Sheffield, Liverpool, Newcastle, Yorkshire, Lancashire, and the wider North of England. You do not need to travel to London to submit your NLV application. The visa requirements and processing are identical at both centres — only the location differs.

Can I use BLS Manchester from Leeds, Sheffield, or Liverpool?

Yes. BLS Manchester covers all of Northern England, including West Yorkshire (Leeds, Bradford, Wakefield), South Yorkshire (Sheffield, Rotherham), and Merseyside (Liverpool, Birkenhead). If you live anywhere in the North of England, BLS Manchester is your designated application centre. You do not need to use BLS London, and there is no benefit in doing so for Northern England residents.

Is BLS Manchester faster than BLS London for NLV applications?

Generally yes. BLS Manchester tends to have shorter appointment wait times and slightly faster processing than BLS London due to lower application volumes. Typical processing from BLS Manchester is 6–12 weeks, compared to 8–14 weeks from London. During peak periods (May to August), the difference is most noticeable. If you have a specific target arrival date in Spain, Manchester applicants tend to have more predictable timelines.

What documents do I need for the Spain NLV from Manchester?

The document requirements are identical for BLS Manchester and BLS London. You need: valid passport (6+ months remaining), completed EX-01 application form, 2 passport photos, ACRO criminal record certificate (apostilled + translated), medical certificate (translated), proof of income (bank statements, pension letters — apostilled + translated), private health insurance certificate from a Spanish-authorised provider, proof of accommodation in Spain (rental contract or title deeds), and the consulate fee payment (€80–€150). All UK documents must be apostilled by the FCDO and translated by a sworn Spanish translator.

I live in Scotland — should I use BLS Manchester?

Many Scottish applicants use BLS Manchester, which covers Scotland as part of its Northern UK territory. There is also a BLS Scotland option for some Scottish postcodes. Always check the current BLS International website to confirm which centre is designated for your specific postcode before booking an appointment. The visa requirements and processing are completely identical at both centres — the only difference is travel convenience.

Can I prove income using my Northern England employer's pension or local authority pension?

Yes. Occupational pension payments from any UK employer — including local authorities (e.g., West Yorkshire Pension Fund, Greater Manchester Pension Fund), NHS, Teachers' Pension, police pension, and private employers — are accepted as passive income for the NLV. You need a statement from the pension provider on official letterhead showing your monthly income amount, plus 3–6 months of bank statements showing the pension deposits. Both the pension letter and bank statements must be apostilled and translated.
