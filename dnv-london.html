Digital Nomad Visa Spain: London Consulate Guide

Your complete guide to applying for Spain's Digital Nomad Visa through the Spanish Consulate General in London. Learn about BLS appointments, required documents, processing times, and the step-by-step application process for England & Wales residents.

Spanish Consulate General in London

Overview of the consulate's role in DNV applications

The Spanish Consulate General in London handles Digital Nomad Visa applications for all residents of England and Wales. The consulate is responsible for reviewing applications, verifying documentation, and issuing visas for eligible applicants.

London consulate has dedicated DNV staff trained in the latest requirements and timelines. The jurisdiction covers:

  • England (all regions)
  • Wales
ℹ️

Scotland & Northern Ireland: Applicants in Scotland apply through the Spanish Consulate General in Edinburgh. Northern Ireland residents should also contact Edinburgh for guidance.

Why Apply Through London?

Applying through the London consulate offers advantages if you're UK-based:

  • No need to travel abroad during the application process (except for final BLS appointment)
  • Familiar UK document requirements (ACRO certificates, HMRC letters, UK apostilles)
  • BLS International has a London office, reducing travel time
  • English-speaking staff familiar with UK documentation standards

However, many London applicants find it faster to apply through the UGE (Unidad de Gestión Exterior) in Spain, which processes DNV applications in approximately 20 days—much quicker than the London consulate's typical 4–12 week timeline.

BLS International London

Outsourced visa processing and appointment booking

What is BLS International?

BLS International is an outsourced visa processing centre contracted by the Spanish government. You don't submit your application directly to the consulate—instead, you book an appointment with BLS, submit your documents there, and BLS forwards everything to the consulate for review.

BLS London Centre Details

Address: BLS International UK Ltd, 39 Kensington High Street, London W8 5NP

Phone: +44 (0) 203 011 0909

Email: info@blsuk.com

Website: www.blsspain-uk.com

How to Book a BLS Appointment

1

Visit BLS Online Portal

Go to the BLS Spain UK website and select "Digital Nomad Visa" as your visa type.

2

Create Account

Register with your email address. You'll receive a confirmation email with login credentials.

3

Complete Application

Fill out the online form with personal information, employment details, and income confirmation.

4

Select Appointment Date

Choose an available date and time. Slots typically fill 2–4 weeks in advance during peak season (spring–summer).

5

Attend Appointment

Bring all original documents, copies, and your application confirmation. BLS staff will verify documents and collect your application.

Appointment Availability & Booking Tips

BLS London is very busy—appointments fill quickly. To maximize your chances:

  • Book early: Create your BLS account and reserve a slot at least 4–6 weeks in advance.
  • Off-peak months: September–November and January–February have fewer applicants and more availability.
  • Walk-in appointments: BLS London does accept walk-ins, but expect long queues. Not recommended unless you're desperate.
  • Set reminders: Slots open daily at specific times. Set calendar alerts to catch new availability.

Appointment Fees

BLS Service Charge: Approximately £10–15 per application

This is in addition to the Spanish visa fee (which is paid separately if your application is approved).

What to Bring to Your BLS Appointment

  • Passport (original + photocopy of photo page)
  • Completed application form (printed or digital)
  • All required original documents (see Documents Checklist section)
  • Photocopies of all documents
  • Proof of BLS appointment booking
  • Any correspondence from the consulate
⚠️

Arrive early and bring originals: BLS staff verify documents on-site. Missing originals will result in appointment cancellation and rescheduling.

Step-by-Step London Application

8-step process from preparation to visa collection

1

Gather UK Documents (Weeks 1–2)

Collect your passport, ACRO certificate (criminal record check), HMRC letters (tax returns or certificate of income), recent UK bank statements (6 months), and employment contracts or client agreements. Request apostilles on documents that require them.

2

Obtain Apostilles (Weeks 2–3)

Submit documents requiring apostilles to the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) or UK government apostille service. Processing typically takes 3–5 working days.

3

Get Documents Translated (Weeks 2–4)

Have any documents not already in English or Spanish translated by a sworn translator (traductor jurado). UK bank statements, ACRO certificates, and employment letters should be translated.

4

Prepare Application (Week 4–5)

Complete the online BLS application form. Ensure your income meets the minimum requirement (€2,442/month gross) and gather bank statements proving consistent income over the past 12 months.

5

Book BLS Appointment (Week 5–6)

Log into the BLS portal and select an available appointment date. Aim for 4–6 weeks out to allow time for document preparation. Confirm the date in writing.

6

Attend BLS Appointment (Week 8–10)

Arrive 15 minutes early with all originals and copies. BLS staff will verify documents, scan your application, and provide a receipt with a reference number for consulate tracking.

7

Wait for Consulate Review (4–12 weeks)

The consulate will review your application. You can track progress using your BLS reference number. Processing varies; some applications are approved within 4 weeks, others take up to 12 weeks.

8

Collect Visa (Upon Approval)

The consulate will notify you when your visa is ready for collection. You can collect it in person at the consulate or have it mailed to your UK address (additional postal fee may apply).

ℹ️

Total Timeline: From document gathering to visa collection typically takes 10–16 weeks, depending on processing speed and how quickly you prepare documents.

Documents Checklist for London Applicants

London-specific requirements and UK documentation rules

Original Documents (Bring to BLS)

  • Passport: Valid for at least 18 months beyond your intended stay in Spain.
  • ACRO Certificate: Criminal record check issued by the UK's Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS). Order online at disclosure.org.uk.
  • HMRC Tax Documents: Either a Certificate of Residence/Tax Return from HMRC or a recent tax year assessment. Request via the HMRC online portal.
  • Bank Statements: 6 months of recent statements from a UK bank showing consistent income and balance (minimum €2,442/month).
  • Employment Contract or Client Agreement: If self-employed, a copy of your contract with clients or a letter from your employer.
  • Health Insurance Documentation: Proof of comprehensive health insurance valid in Spain (see Insurance section).

Apostille Requirements (UK Documents)

The following UK documents must have an apostille (UK government certification):

  • ACRO Certificate (criminal record)
  • HMRC Certificate of Residence (if applicable)
  • Diplomas or educational certificates (if you have them)
  • UK-issued marriage or birth certificates (if applicable)

How to get UK apostilles:

  • For ACRO certificates: The DBS automatically apostilles DBS-issued documents. Request the apostilled version when ordering.
  • For HMRC documents: Submit to the FCDO Apostille Office, Hanslope Park, Northampton NN4 0EF or apply online via the UK government apostille service.
  • Processing time: 3–5 working days for UK apostilles.
  • Cost: £30–40 per document.

Translation Requirements (Sworn Translator)

UK documents must be translated into Spanish by a traductor jurado (sworn translator approved by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs). Required translations include:

  • ACRO Certificate (English → Spanish)
  • HMRC Tax Documents (English → Spanish)
  • Bank Statements (English → Spanish)
  • Employment Contract (English → Spanish)

Finding a sworn translator:

  • Search the official list at: Spanish Ministry of Justice website
  • Look for translators in London or UK with English→Spanish accreditation
  • Cost typically ranges from £15–30 per document

Pro Tips for London Applicants

  • Order everything early: ACRO and HMRC documents can take weeks. Start the moment you decide to apply.
  • Double-check apostilles: Spanish consulates are strict about apostille quality. Ensure the apostille is genuine and legible.
  • Translate everything consistently: Use the same sworn translator for all documents to maintain formatting consistency.
  • Get extra copies: Make 2–3 photocopies of every document. Consulates sometimes request duplicates.
  • Use a checklist: Print a document checklist and tick off each item before your BLS appointment.

Processing Times from London

Typical timelines and factors affecting approval speed

Typical Processing Timeline

From BLS submission to visa approval: 4–12 weeks

However, timelines vary significantly:

  • Fast track (4–6 weeks): Complete applications with all documents, no requests for additional information.
  • Standard (6–8 weeks): Most applications fall into this range.
  • Slow track (8–12 weeks): Applications with incomplete documentation or consulate requests for clarification.

Factors That Speed Up Processing

  • Submitting a 100% complete application with all documents on the first attempt
  • Clear bank statements showing consistent income for 12+ months
  • Proper apostilles and translations (no rejections for formatting)
  • Simple employment structure (direct client agreements are clearer than complex contract chains)
  • Early submission (applications submitted in off-peak months process faster)

Factors That Slow Processing

  • Missing or incorrect apostilles
  • Poor quality translations or translator not on Spain's official list
  • Unclear income documentation (irregular payments, multiple income sources)
  • Consulate requesting additional documents (can add 2–4 weeks)
  • Applying during peak season (April–July) when consulate is overwhelmed

What to Do If Processing Delays Occur

After 8 weeks without update: Contact BLS with your reference number. They can request a status check from the consulate.

After 12 weeks: Escalate to the consulate directly. You have the right to know your application status.

If documents are requested: The consulate will email you via BLS. Respond within 10–14 days to avoid application rejection.

⚠️

Important: Do not submit a new application or book a new BLS appointment if you're waiting for consulate feedback. Contact the consulate directly first.

London vs Spain: Two Routes Compared

Why many London applicants apply through the UGE instead

You have two options for applying as a UK resident:

  • Option 1: Apply through London Consulate (4–12 weeks)
  • Option 2: Apply through UGE in Spain (20 days)

Many London applicants choose to apply through Spain's UGE because it's significantly faster. Here's how the routes compare:

London Consulate Route

Processing Time: 4–12 weeks

Cost: BLS fee (~£10–15) + apostille/translation costs (~£200–400)

Pros:

  • No travel required (except BLS appointment)
  • UK documents don't need certification again
  • Familiar with UK documentation standards

Cons:

  • Much slower than UGE
  • Consulate frequently requests additional documents
  • Limited visibility into application progress

Spain UGE Route

Processing Time: ~20 days

Cost: UGE fee (~€80–100) + apostille/translation costs (~£200–400)

Pros:

  • Much faster (20 days vs 4–12 weeks)
  • Direct processing—no BLS intermediary
  • Real-time tracking of application status
  • Professional visa agent can guide you

Cons:

  • Requires travel to Spain (or working with a visa agent)
  • Documents may need re-certification in Spain
  • Requires Spanish bank account or payment method

Which Route Should You Choose?

Choose London if:

  • You're not in a hurry (4–12 weeks is acceptable)
  • You can't travel to Spain during application process
  • You want to use UK BLS for convenience

Choose Spain UGE if:

  • You want your visa within 20 days
  • You can travel to Spain or hire a visa agent to handle submission
  • You want faster, more transparent processing

UK Consulate Jurisdiction Map

Where to apply based on your UK residence

Apply in London if you live in:

  • England: All regions (London, Southeast, Southwest, Midlands, North, Northeast, etc.)
  • Wales: All regions (Cardiff, Swansea, etc.)

Apply in Edinburgh if you live in:

  • Scotland: All regions (Edinburgh, Glasgow, Dundee, etc.)
  • Northern Ireland: Contact Edinburgh consulate for guidance (some applicants apply in Belfast, but Edinburgh is the primary contact)
ℹ️

Temporary residence in different region? Apply through the consulate corresponding to your permanent address or where you hold a residential lease. If you're moving house during the application, notify the consulate immediately.

Can You Apply Through a Different Consulate?

No. You must apply through the consulate corresponding to your residence. However, if you're planning to move to Scotland or relocate, some applicants have successfully requested a transfer to the appropriate consulate. Contact BLS or the consulate to discuss.

Pro Tips for London Applicants

Best practices to speed up your application

Document Preparation

  • Start ordering documents now. ACRO certificates can take 3–4 weeks. HMRC documents may take 1–2 weeks. Don't wait.
  • Request apostilles upfront. Order apostilles the moment you receive documents. UK apostille processing takes 3–5 days but can back up during peak season.
  • Use a UK-approved sworn translator. Verify that your translator appears on the Spanish Ministry of Justice list (MJUSTICIA) for English→Spanish translation. Consulates reject applications from unknown translators.
  • Get multiple copies. Print 5 copies of every document. Consulates sometimes lose copies or request duplicates during review.

BLS Appointment

  • Book 6 weeks in advance. BLS appointments fill quickly. Set a calendar alert for when new slots open and book immediately.
  • Avoid peak season. Booking in September–October or January–February means shorter queues and faster processing.
  • Arrive 20 minutes early. BLS can be chaotic. Arriving early gives you time to find parking, use the bathroom, and prepare your documents.
  • Bring everything in a folder. Organize documents by category (identification, income, criminal record, health insurance). This speeds up verification.

Application Completeness

  • Quadruple-check the application form. Errors in your BLS form lead to consulate requests for clarification, which adds weeks. Review every field before submitting.
  • Ensure income clarity. If self-employed, make sure 12 months of bank statements clearly show income deposits. If employed, ensure your contract and HMRC documents are consistent.
  • Health insurance is non-negotiable. Have proof of comprehensive Spain-valid insurance ready. Consulate will reject applications without it.

After Submission

  • Keep your BLS reference number. Write it down immediately after your appointment. You'll need it to track progress and contact the consulate.
  • Check email weekly. The consulate may request additional documents via BLS email. If you miss the notification, your application may be delayed or rejected.
  • Be responsive. If the consulate requests documents, respond within 7–10 days. Delays on your end reset the timeline.
  • Ask for status updates after 8 weeks. If you haven't heard back, contact BLS to request a status check from the consulate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from London applicants

How far in advance should I book my BLS appointment?

We recommend booking 6 weeks in advance. During peak season (April–July), appointments fill 8–10 weeks out. If you're applying in off-peak months (September–February), you may get slots 2–4 weeks out. Create your BLS account as early as possible—you can book the moment you're ready with all documents.

What happens if the consulate requests additional documents?

The consulate will email you via BLS with a list of additional documents needed. You typically have 10–14 days to respond. Submit the requested documents through the BLS portal or by mail. Processing restarts once the consulate receives them, adding another 2–4 weeks to your timeline. This is one reason why submitting a complete application the first time is critical.

Can I collect my visa in person at the consulate, or must I wait for it to be mailed?

The consulate will notify you when your visa is ready. You can collect it in person at the Spanish Consulate General in London (1 Montagu Place, London W1H 2AL) during business hours. Alternatively, they can mail it to your UK address for a small postal fee. Personal collection is faster and avoids postal delays.

What if my processing takes longer than 12 weeks?

If your application hasn't been approved after 12 weeks, contact BLS with your reference number and request a status update from the consulate. Extended delays sometimes happen due to staff holidays or high application volume. You have the right to know where your application stands. If the consulate requests additional information, respond immediately to avoid further delays.

Do I need to resubmit my DNV application if my circumstances change?

If your employment or income changes significantly during processing, notify the consulate immediately. Minor changes (like a new client) don't require resubmission. However, if your income drops below €2,442/month or your employment ends, you may need to reapply. Contact BLS to discuss your specific situation.

Which consulate applies to me: London or Edinburgh?

If you live in England or Wales, apply through London. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland, apply through Edinburgh. Your jurisdiction is determined by your permanent residence address (not where you're visiting). If you're in the process of moving, apply through the consulate for your current address and notify them of your planned move.

Is expedited processing available through London Consulate?

The London Consulate does not offer formal expedited processing (unlike some other visa types). However, Spain's UGE offers standard processing in 20 days. If speed is a priority, many applicants find it faster to apply through the UGE in Spain or hire a visa agent. See our UK vs Spain route comparison guide for details.

Can I apply through Manchester instead of London?

There is no Spanish Consulate in Manchester. England is served by the London consulate (with BLS in London). Manchester residents must apply through London. However, some applicants choose the faster UGE route in Spain if they don't want to travel to London for a BLS appointment. See our Manchester applicant guide for alternative options.

How do I book an appointment at the Spanish Consulate in London?

Appointments are booked through the consulate's online appointment system at the Spanish Embassy London website. During peak periods, appointments book out 6-10 weeks in advance. Check availability as soon as you start preparing documents.

What criminal record certificate do UK applicants need?

UK applicants must obtain an ACRO Police Certificate — not a standard DBS check. ACRO covers the entire UK police database and meets the Spanish consulate requirement. Apply at acro.police.uk. The certificate needs to be apostilled by the FCDO and sworn translated into Spanish.

How long does the FCDO apostille take?

The FCDO apostille service typically takes 2-3 weeks by post. Faster turnaround is available through registered solicitors at higher cost. Budget 3-4 weeks minimum when planning your application timeline.

Can I apply for the Digital Nomad Visa from within Spain instead?

Yes. If you're already legally in Spain, you can apply for the DNV from within Spain at the Oficina de Extranjeros (UGE). This in-country route grants a 3-year initial residence authorisation rather than 1 year, and avoids the consulate appointment wait.

Does the Beckham Law apply to UK nationals on the DNV?

Yes. UK nationals who obtain the Digital Nomad Visa and register in Spain can apply for the Beckham Law — a flat 24% tax rate on Spanish-source income up to €600,000 for up to 6 years. Apply via Form 149 within 6 months of registering with Spanish social security.

What income level do I need for the London DNV application?

The income threshold is approximately €2,442/month (200% of the Spanish SMI in 2026). For UK applicants, this is roughly £2,200+/month using typical exchange rates. Bank statements and employer documentation must clearly show this level of consistent income.
