Common Questions Frequently Asked Questions
How long does the eligibility check take?

The pathway selector above takes under a minute. Once you register on your chosen dashboard, the full eligibility check takes around five minutes to complete.

Is the eligibility check free?

Yes. The eligibility check is completely free and carries no obligation to proceed with a paid service. We only ask you to continue if we have confirmed you are a strong candidate for the visa.

What if none of the options match my situation?

Choose “I am not sure yet” and book a free consultation. Our specialists deal with complex and non-standard situations regularly — we will work out the right path with you.

What happens after I complete the eligibility check?

You will receive a result confirming whether you appear to qualify for your chosen visa route. A specialist will follow up to confirm your eligibility, explain the application process, and give you a clear quote before any work begins.

Can family members apply together?

Yes. Most Spain visa routes allow dependants (spouses and children) to be included in the application. Let us know your family situation when you register and we will advise on how to proceed.

I have already been refused. Can you still help?

Yes. Start the eligibility check as normal and note your previous refusal. We assess each case individually and advise whether an appeal or a fresh application is the stronger route.
