Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about the checklist and NLV process

What documents are included in the NLV checklist?

The checklist covers every required document for a Spanish Non-Lucrative Visa application: valid passport, biometric photos, criminal record certificate (with apostille and sworn translation), medical certificate (with apostille and sworn translation), proof of financial means, compliant health insurance, proof of accommodation in Spain, and the completed EX-01 application form. It also includes a timeline section so you know when to prepare each document.

Do all documents need to be translated into Spanish?

Yes. Any document not originally in Spanish must be accompanied by a certified sworn translation (traducción jurada) carried out by an officially recognised translator. The checklist clearly marks which documents require translation and what format the translation must take for each consulate.

How long are NLV documents valid for?

Criminal record certificates are generally valid for 3 months from issue date. Medical certificates are valid for 3 months. Bank statements should cover the 3–6 months prior to application. The checklist includes validity windows alongside each document so you can time your preparation correctly and avoid having to re-order documents.

Can I use this checklist for a family application?

Yes. The checklist includes a dedicated section for family member (dependant) documents, including additional criminal record certificates, marriage certificates, birth certificates for children, and how financial requirements change when including dependants. Each family member over 16 will need their own criminal record check.
