Health Insurance

Family Health Insurance in Spain

Covering a partner and children in Spain usually means one family arrangement or several individual policies. This guide explains how family cover works, what visa applications need from each family member, and how to get a family quote — with or without a visa.

Get My Health Insurance Quote

Individual vs Family Policies

Sanitas family cover can include a spouse or partner, children and sometimes other dependants on one arrangement. Each person is assessed on their own age and health, so a family quote is built person by person rather than as one flat price. Some couples choose one family arrangement and others two individual policies — we can quote both so you can compare.

Covering a Spouse or Partner

A spouse or partner can usually be included, assessed on their own age and medical history. If one of you has a medical condition, the outcome can differ between you: one person may be accepted on standard terms while the other has an exclusion or a higher premium.

Children and Newborns

Children can usually be included on family cover, each assessed through the health declaration. Whether and when a newborn can be added depends on the current Sanitas process and your policy, so tell us your due date as early as possible and it’s confirmed with your quote. We don’t publish child prices: each child is priced in your personalised quote.

Pregnancy and Maternity

If you’re pregnant or planning a pregnancy, say so when you ask for a quote. Maternity cover and any timing rules depend on the plan and on current Sanitas terms, so we confirm what applies to you before you buy.

What Consulates Require for Family Applications

For Non-Lucrative and Digital Nomad family applications, every applicant — including children — needs health insurance. That can be individual policies or one family policy, as long as the certificate names each person. The same applies to family members who join a student in higher education. Families on a visa route are usually quoted Sanitas Residents or Residents Platinum. You can arrange your health insurance through us even if you apply for your visa yourself or use another provider.

Families Already Living in Spain or Registering as EU Citizens

If you don’t need cover for a visa, you can choose between plans with or without co-payments: a plan with co-payments can cost less, while a no-copay plan keeps costs predictable. Families registering residency as EU citizens are usually quoted the no-copay Más Salud Familias range — see health insurance for EU citizens.

What Affects a Family Premium

Each person’s age and health, where you live in Spain, the plan and its co-payment level, and how many people are covered. Every family quote is personalised, so we don’t publish family or child prices.

You don’t need a visa — or our visa service — to buy health insurance through us.
