Private Insurance is Mandatory

Private health insurance is not optional for the Digital Nomad Visa. Spain's immigration authority requires all DNV applicants to hold a valid private health insurance policy that meets strict criteria. Without compliant coverage, your application will be rejected.

⚠️

Critical: Your insurance certificate must explicitly show zero co-payment (sin copago) and comprehensive coverage across Spain. Policies that don't meet these requirements will lead to visa rejection.

What Makes a Policy Visa-Compliant?

✓

✓

✓

✓

✓
In force for the period you apply for — Renew the policy without a break while you hold the permit.

DNV Holders & Spanish Social Security

The relationship between private insurance and Spanish Social Security (Seguridad Social) can be confusing. Here's the reality:

If you work for a foreign company (remote):

You are not contributing to Spanish Social Security. Private insurance is required for your visa application and is your only access to healthcare in Spain.

If you register as autónomo (self-employed):

Once you contribute to Spanish Social Security, you gain access to public healthcare. Check with us whether your route still needs private insurance documents. Many DNV holders with autónomo status keep a private policy anyway for flexibility and shorter wait times.

The key point:

Most applicants need private insurance documents for the application. If you will be registered with Spanish Social Security, the UGE may not need them — we confirm your route before you buy.

ℹ️

Tax and health coverage are separate. Getting a Spanish tax ID (NIF) does not change the insurance requirement for the DNV application; Social Security registration can, so check your route with us.

Pre-Existing Conditions: What Happens?

Many DNV applicants worry about how insurers handle pre-existing medical conditions. Here's how the three outcomes work:

Option 1: Excluded (Most Common)

The insurer issues your policy but excludes the condition from cover. Ask how the exclusion will appear on your certificate before you apply, and plan for that condition not being covered.

Option 2: Rejected

The insurer declines to cover the condition and refuses to issue a policy without excluding it. This is rare with major Spanish insurers but can happen with smaller providers. Raise this before you apply, so your visa timing is not affected.

Option 3: Accepted with Premium Increase

The insurer includes the condition with no exclusions but charges a higher premium (typically 15–50% more). This option appears on your visa certificate and proves you have full coverage, including for pre-existing conditions.

Before you apply: Disclose your medical history fully and ask how any exclusion will appear on your certificate. Complex medical cases take time, so start early.

Travel Insurance vs. Health Insurance: The Critical Difference

Travel insurance DOES NOT qualify for the DNV.

Travel insurance is short-term emergency coverage designed for holidays and trips. It typically covers accidents abroad but excludes routine care, chronic conditions, and prescription medications. Immigration authorities will immediately reject any DNV application submitted with travel insurance.

You need proper Spanish private health insurance:

  • Full comprehensive coverage (doctor visits, hospital stays, prescriptions)
  • Valid for your entire visa period (1–3 years)
  • No-copay certificate explicitly stated
  • Issued by a Spain-authorised insurer

If you already have travel insurance, you'll need to purchase a separate private health policy for the visa application. Many expats maintain both, but immigration only requires the private policy.

How Buying Your Policy Works

What happens between asking for a quote and handing your certificate to the consulate:

  1. Get a quote. Tell Spanish Health Insurance who is applying, each person’s age and where you will live in Spain.
  2. Answer the health questionnaire. Each applicant declares their medical history. The insurer decides whether to accept, exclude or price any condition, so declare everything — an undisclosed condition can affect your cover later.
  3. Choose the start date — normally your planned arrival date, running for the period you apply for.
  4. Pay and receive the policy. Premiums are usually paid annually or in instalments; some consulates ask to see the year paid upfront, so we check what yours expects.
  5. Get your certificate. It should be in Spanish and name each applicant, the policy dates, full cover in Spain, no co-payments and no waiting periods. It goes in with your visa application.

When to buy: once your appointment date is known — early enough to have the certificate for the appointment, without paying for months of cover before you travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about health insurance for the Digital Nomad Visa in Spain.

Is health insurance mandatory for the Digital Nomad Visa in Spain?

Yes, private health insurance is a mandatory requirement for the Digital Nomad Visa application. Your policy must meet specific criteria including no co-payment coverage and full comprehensive coverage across Spain, or your application will be rejected.

What does 'sin copago' (no co-payment) mean for the DNV?

Sin copago means zero out-of-pocket costs at the point of care. Your insurance certificate must explicitly show no co-payment requirement. This means when you visit a doctor or hospital, you pay nothing — the insurer covers 100% of the cost (up to plan limits). This must appear on the official document submitted to immigration.

Can I use travel insurance for the Digital Nomad Visa?

No. Travel insurance does not qualify for the Digital Nomad Visa. You must have proper Spanish private health insurance that meets the specific visa requirements, including no co-payment and comprehensive coverage across Spain. Travel insurance is short-term emergency coverage and will be rejected by immigration authorities.

What if I register as autónomo and contribute to Spanish Social Security?

If you're registered as autónomo (self-employed) and contribute to Spanish Social Security, you gain access to public healthcare. However, your visa application still requires private insurance coverage. Many DNV holders with autónomo status keep a private policy anyway for flexibility and shorter wait times than public healthcare.

How much does health insurance cost for the Digital Nomad Visa?

The price depends mainly on age, location and medical history, and usually rises with age. A personalised quote is the only reliable figure.

What happens to my application if I have pre-existing conditions?

Insurers typically offer one of three options: issue a policy with the condition excluded (the exclusion doesn't show on your visa certificate), reject the policy, or accept it with a higher premium. Ask how any exclusion would appear on your certificate before you apply.

How long does it take to get a policy and visa certificate?

Quotes are usually quick, and the insurance certificate you need for your visa application is issued once the policy is in place.

Do I need to renew my insurance during my visa period?

Your insurance must be valid for the entire duration of your visa period. If your visa is valid for 3 years, your insurance must cover all 3 years (or be renewable). Most policies are annual and can be renewed. Check with your insurer about renewal procedures before your coverage ends.

Does my UK or US health insurance count for the DNV?

No. Your home country health insurance generally does not satisfy the Spanish DNV health insurance requirement. You need a policy that provides full medical coverage in Spain without copayments, from a provider recognised as operating in Spain or the EU.

What is the no-copayment requirement for DNV health insurance?

Spanish visa health insurance must have no copayments (copagos) — meaning no per-visit charges, no co-insurance percentages, and no excess payments for treatment. Many basic health insurance plans include copayments (a set fee per GP visit or specialist consultation). These are not accepted for visa applications.

Can I use international health insurance for the DNV?

Only if it meets the same conditions as any other policy: comprehensive cover in Spain, no co-payments and no waiting periods. The UGE does not accept travel insurance or refund-only policies. Check the certificate wording before you apply.

How much does DNV health insurance cost per month?

The price depends mainly on age, location and medical history, and usually rises with age. A personalised quote is the only reliable figure.

Do I need to maintain health insurance throughout the DNV period?

Yes. Health insurance must be maintained continuously throughout your DNV residence period. For renewals, you'll need to demonstrate continued insurance coverage. Allowing your policy to lapse can jeopardise your renewal application.
